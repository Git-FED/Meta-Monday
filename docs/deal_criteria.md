# Deal criteria

A candidate can be labeled `confirmed` only when all required checks pass. Otherwise use `needs_live_check`, `uncertain`, or `rejected`.

## Required checks

1. **Identity:** product, model, condition, and variant are unambiguous.
2. **Live listing:** a canonical URL resolves to the relevant product or offer page.
3. **Current price:** the payable item price is recorded with currency and timestamp.
4. **Total cost:** shipping, mandatory fees, membership requirements, and coupon requirements are recorded when known.
5. **Availability:** the item is in stock or the listing explicitly supports ordering.
6. **Merchant quality:** seller, fulfillment, return policy, and warranty risks are noted.
7. **Value evidence:** at least one of the following is present:
   - at least 15% below a reliable recent or historical price;
   - materially below two or more comparable legitimate listings;
   - a documented bundle or included benefit that makes the total value superior.
8. **Terms:** quantity limits, expiry, regional restrictions, subscription terms, and required payment method are recorded.

## Rejection conditions

Reject or hold for review when the listing is counterfeit-risk, unavailable, materially different from the comparison item, price-error bait, coupon-only without the coupon, inaccessible without bypassing controls, or missing enough evidence to reproduce the claim.

## Suggested score

Use a 0–100 score as a review aid, not as proof:

- 30 points: price/value advantage;
- 20 points: confidence in price history or cross-merchant comparison;
- 15 points: stock and checkout availability;
- 15 points: merchant, returns, and warranty quality;
- 10 points: low friction (no membership, coupon, or complex terms);
- 10 points: freshness of verification.

A score of 75 or higher is a strong review candidate. It is not authorization to purchase.
